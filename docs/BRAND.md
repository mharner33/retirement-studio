# Brand

## Fonts

From Google Fonts:

- **Headings and body**: Instrument Sans
- **Accent (a tagline or a quote)**: Instrument Serif
- **Code, numbers and identifiers**: JetBrains Mono

## Colors
User interface should have a light and a dark theme option with the following:

| Role                      | Light theme                | Dark theme                 |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Page background           | Ice blue #E6EBF4           | Deep midnight #191B2E      |
| Sidebar                   | Pale periwinkle #CBD5FE    | Midnight slate #27293B     |
| Cards and tables          | Frost white #F7F9FF        | Midnight slate #27293B     |
| Primary text              | Midnight slate #27293B     | Ice blue #E6EBF4           |
| Secondary text            | Muted slate #626577        | Light slate #ADB3CC        |
| Primary button            | #32386C fill, #F7F9FF text | #A8B1EB fill, #191B2E text |
| Selected navigation       | #CAD1FD fill, #27293B text | #32386C fill, #E6EBF4 text |
| Focus ring                | Violet-blue #4F58AA        | Dusty periwinkle #A8B1EB   |
| Card borders and dividers | #CBD5FE                    | #414665                    |


Panels are rounded with no outline. Sea Green and Marigold are too bright for small text, so darken them for headings and body text on light backgrounds and lighten them on dark ones.

## Avoid

- Borders on one edge of a panel, and thin lines across the top of the page
- Heavy use of gradient fills

## Tone

Plain business English in US spelling. Short labels, no jargon and no exclamation marks. Money is in US dollars, such as $1,250.00.
